## What was asked, and how it was measured

Find the best combination of classic ML models (a soft vote) and the best neural net, make them the defaults, and retrain. Script: `nn_ml_comb_test.py`.

**Protocol -- the same as both trainers.** All recordings of all five wearers, `affine` per-wearer calibration, window-level dedup, and every fold holds out ONE PERSON: trained on four, scored on the fifth. So every number below is *on a person the model never saw* (who calibrated first -- CLAUDE.md 8k).

**ML.** 16 classifiers (LDA, logistic regression, RBF-SVM at four C values, random forest and extra trees at two leaf sizes, gradient boosting, kNN at two k, two MLPs) on every feature setup (signal representation x feature set x window length). Every model's out-of-fold probabilities are stored, so every soft vote can be scored without refitting: greedy forward selection plus every subset of 2-4 of the ten best singles, per setup. The vote is unweighted because the deployed `VotingClassifier` is.

**Two ML numbers, and the second is the one to quote.** Picking the best of hundreds of combinations on the same five folds is optimistic. The NESTED check redoes the whole choice (setup AND members) without each person, then scores it on that person only.

**NN.** The three best architectures of the previous sweep x window lengths 0.5-1.5 s in 0.2 s steps, 150 epochs (N1); width / dropout / learning-rate variants of the best four (N2); two extra seeds for the best two, ranked by the mean (N3). The net never votes with the ML models (safety rule 13).

**NN search stopped early, by choice:** stopped after stage N1 (12 of 34 configurations: gru and cnn_gru x 0.5-1.5 s; tcn, the N2 variants and the N3 seeds were not run). GRU at 0.7 s was kept as the net -- one seed, so its score has no seed spread.
